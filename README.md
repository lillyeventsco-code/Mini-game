# Pip's Garden

A tiny, cozy gardening game for iPhone. Plant seeds, water sprouts, pick flowers, and keep Pip the hedgehog company.

## How to play

- **Tap empty soil** to plant the selected seed.
- **Tap a sprout** to water it. Keep tapping to make it grow faster (up to 5x speed); the boost fades a moment after you stop. The droplet shows how much water is left. Plants never wilt; they just pause until watered.
- **Tap a bloom** to pick it for acorns, and sometimes seeds back.
- **Tap Pip** for a pat. Once a day, Pip finds you 5 acorns.
- Spend acorns in the **Seed shop** on new seeds and garden patches. Picking a flower unlocks the next kind.
- The sky follows your real clock: sunrise, sunset, fireflies at night. Moonpetals are worth double after dark.
- Every so often a rain shower waters everything for you.

Progress saves automatically on the device.

## Put it on your iPhone

1. Host the folder with GitHub Pages: repo **Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch**, pick the branch and `/ (root)`.
2. Open the Pages URL in Safari on your iPhone.
3. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. It opens full screen like an app.

No build step: it is a single `index.html` with no dependencies.
